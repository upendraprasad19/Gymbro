VICKY.

From coaching others
to winning Mr. Jharkhand.

Vicky grew up with limited means and a strong pull toward martial arts and fitness. He built a career coaching others in local gyms.

During that career, he travelled to Uttarakhand to learn MMA and kickboxing, and participated in a fight. He returned, entered bodybuilding and won Mr. Jharkhand.
