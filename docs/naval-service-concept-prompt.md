## Final prompt

Use case: identity-preserve / photorealistic-natural. Create a NEW landscape 3:2 editorial photograph for a clearly labelled fictional AI naval-service concept on a fitness founder's story page. Input image is an AI-generated fictional man used as a placeholder, NOT the actual founder or an actual service photograph. Keep this same fictional man's facial structure, skin tone, hair and athletic build. Change the scene and clothes: standing naturally on an open ship deck at sea, one hand lightly resting on a grey metal rail, three-quarter view, looking toward the horizon rather than posing at the camera. Plain crisp white short-sleeved collared naval-style working shirt and plain white trousers, no cap, no visible rank stripes, medals, name tag, official emblem, insignia, vessel name, flags or logos. Calm deep blue ocean and a restrained out-of-focus grey ship structure, soft early morning daylight, cool steel-blue atmosphere, natural warm skin tones, fabric creases and realistic skin texture. Frame from mid-thigh upward, subject slightly right of centre with generous headroom and usable surrounding sea/ship context, so a diagonal CSS crop will not cut the head or face. Documentary restraint, understated and human. No salute, weapons, combat, action-movie effects, victory pose, artificial glowing rim light, typographic overlays, montage, UI, decorative frame, borders or watermark. Output a single full photographic scene, not a collage. This is illustrative naval context, not a reconstruction of a real historical event.
